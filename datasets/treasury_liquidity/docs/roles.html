Roles

Agreed 20 October 2026

This document defines three separate sets of roles:

  1. Consumer uses: what the datasets will be used for.
  2. API access roles: who may call the API and what each caller can see.
  3. Business roles in the data: the fictional people inside the firm, and what each may do.

1. Consumer uses

Each use reads the same core data. Anything specific to one use goes in a use pack under datasets/<name>/packs/<use>/. A new use adds a pack and does not change the core data.

2. API access roles

Locally, the caller chooses a role with an X-Role header plus a dev key held in the Worker's local secrets file. No key means public.

The API is read-only for every role. You rebuild the data by running the Python generator, not through the API. The plan's admin role is dropped because it would have nothing to do.

3. Business roles in the data (treasury liquidity dataset)

Agreed 20 October 2026

The people at Thameside Bank who prepare, review and sign off the liquidity forecast, and the committee they report to. They are in the staff table, and are entries TFG-057 to TFG-062 in the firm-wide staff register, with the Chief Financial Officer (TFG-001) as chair of ALCO. The group's approval limits do not fit wholesale dealing, so dealers have per-deal dealing limits instead.

RoleJob title in the dataDealing limit per dealDuties

Money markets dealerMoney Markets Dealer£50,000,000Agrees wholesale deposits, certificates of deposit and repos within the limit, and books each deal in the dealing system the same day.

Head of treasuryHead of Treasury£250,000,000Signs off the ladder for ALCO, owns the liquidity policy, and reports limit breaches. Commissioned the quarter's ladder for ALCO on 6 October.
Liquidity risk managerLiquidity Risk Manager (Risk, not Treasury)NoneSets the stress assumptions and reviews the stress results independently of Treasury. May not change the forecast.
Chair of ALCOChief Financial OfficerNone; ALCO approves deals above £250,000,000Chairs the Asset and Liability Committee, which sets the limits, meets on the first Tuesday of each month and receives every breach report.

Rules on who may do what, which the planted cases test:

  • The base case holds only flows from base-case runs. Stress runs are for the stress ladders only.
  • An inflow counts in the base case only when a contract fixes it.
  • Every deal booked is in the forecast by the next run.
  • A limit breach is reported to ALCO the same day, by the Head of Treasury.
