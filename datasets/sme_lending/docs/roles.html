Roles

Agreed 20 October 2026

This document defines three separate sets of roles:

  1. Consumer uses: what the datasets will be used for.
  2. API access roles: who may call the API and what each caller can see.
  3. Business roles in the data: the fictional people inside the firm, and what each may do.

1. Consumer uses

Each use reads the same core data. Anything specific to one use goes in a use pack under datasets/<name>/packs/<use>/. A new use adds a pack and does not change the core data.

The API is read-only for every role. You rebuild the data by running the Python generator, not through the API. The plan's admin role is dropped because it would have nothing to do.

3. Business roles in the data (SME lending dataset)

Agreed 20 October 2026

The people at Thameside Bank who prepare and decide business loan applications. They are in the staff table, and are entries TFG-039, TFG-040 and TFG-051 to TFG-056 in the firm-wide staff register. Lending authority reuses the group's £100,000 and £250,000 approval limits. The automated scorecard is not a person, but it decides some applications, so its limits are listed too.

RoleJob title in the dataLending authorityDuties
Relationship managerRelationship Manager, Business BankingNoneLooks after a portfolio of businesses. Takes the application, collects the accounts and security details, and prepares the application summary. May not decide any application.
Automated scorecard(a system, not in staff)£50,000Grades every application. Decides online applications for unsecured loans up to £50,000, only when no referral test applies.
Credit managerCredit Manager£100,000Checks the summary against the records and decides applications within authority, including referred ones.
Senior credit managerSenior Credit Manager£250,000Decides larger applications and referrals within authority. Prepares papers for the Credit Committee.
Head of business banking creditHead of Business Banking Credit£1,000,000, as chair of the Credit CommitteeChairs the Credit Committee, which meets on Tuesdays and decides applications above £250,000 and every policy exception. Owns the lending policy. Commissioned the review of the quarter's applications.

Rules on who may do what, which the planted cases test:

  • Every figure in the summary follows the lending policy's definitions. The person deciding checks the summary against the records.
  • An application that fails a test whose outcome is Decline is declined, unless the Credit Committee approves it as a recorded policy exception.
  • An application in a referral band is decided by a person with enough authority, never automatically.
  • No decision is made on out-of-date accounts or valuations. The application waits for new ones.
