Roles

Agreed 20 October 2026

This document defines three separate sets of roles:

  1. Consumer uses: what the datasets will be used for.
  2. API access roles: who may call the API and what each caller can see.
  3. Business roles in the data: the fictional people inside the firm, and what each may do.

1. Consumer uses

Each use reads the same core data. Anything specific to one use goes in a use pack under datasets/<name>/packs/<use>/. A new use adds a pack and does not change the core data.

The API is read-only for every role. You rebuild the data by running the Python generator, not through the API. The plan's admin role is dropped because it would have nothing to do.

3. Business roles in the data (insurance claims dataset)

Agreed 20 October 2026

The people at Thameside Insurance who assess claims, approve settlements and declines, check suspicious claims and handle complaints. They are in the staff table, and are entries TFG-041 to TFG-050 in the firm-wide staff register. Settlements follow an authority ladder that reuses the group's approval limits.

RoleJob title in the dataSettlement authorityDuties
Claims handlerClaims Handler£5,000Takes the claim, checks cover and evidence against the policy wording, agrees costs with suppliers, sets the reserve and proposes the settlement. Approves settlements within authority. Cannot decline a claim.
Team leaderClaims Team Leader£25,000Approves larger settlements and every decline. Checks that the clause cited fits the facts.
Claims managerClaims Manager£100,000Approves the largest settlements and runs the claims team day to day.
Head of claimsHead of Claims£250,000Owns claims handling, the supplier panel and the settlement target. Commissioned the September review.
Claims validationClaims Validation InvestigatorNoneReviews claims referred for possible fraud or error: incidents before cover, duplicate or overlapping invoices, missing evidence.
Complaints handlerComplaints HandlerNoneHandles complaints under the FCA's complaint-handling rules. Reviews a complaint about a decline against the wording and the evidence, and agrees redress.
Customer outcomesCustomer Outcomes ManagerNoneMonitors claims and complaints outcomes for Consumer Duty and reports them to the board.

Rules on who may do what, which the planted cases test:

  • A settlement is provisional until someone whose authority covers it approves it. Only then is it a final decision.
  • A settlement is proposed only when the evidence the policy requires has been received.
  • A claim is declined only under a clause that fits the facts and the evidence, and only by a Claims Team Leader or above.
  • A supplier invoice is checked against the supplier's other invoices before it is paid.
